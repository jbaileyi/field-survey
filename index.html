Preferences

Privacy and storage

  • Photos and results are stored only on this device, in the browser database.
  • Photos are sent to Google's Gemini API only when you tap Analyze. No other server is involved, and the app has no analytics or third-party code.
  • Free-tier Gemini keys may allow Google to use submitted content to improve products. Use a paid or billing-enabled key if the photos are sensitive.
  • Restrict your key in Google AI Studio or Cloud Console, and delete it there if a phone is lost.
